AETNA CLINICAL POLICY BULLETIN
Prior Authorization Criteria for Adalimumab (Humira)
Policy Number: 0578 | Effective Date: 2024-01-01
Applies to: Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)

---

Criterion 1: Confirmed Diagnosis
The member must have a confirmed diagnosis of moderate-to-severe active rheumatoid arthritis (RA), defined as:
- 6 or more swollen joints AND 6 or more tender joints, OR
- DAS28-CRP score >= 3.2 (moderate disease activity), OR
- Physician attestation of moderate-to-severe disease activity
Applicable ICD-10 codes: M05.xx (Seropositive rheumatoid arthritis), M06.xx (Other rheumatoid arthritis)
The diagnosis must be confirmed by a board-certified rheumatologist or specialist.

---

Criterion 2: Prescriber Qualification
Adalimumab must be prescribed by or in consultation with a board-certified rheumatologist or a specialist with documented expertise in the treatment of inflammatory arthritis. Primary care prescriptions are acceptable only with documented rheumatology consultation on file.

---

Criterion 3: Prior DMARD Therapy — Step Therapy Requirement
The member must have documented inadequate response to, or intolerance of, at least ONE conventional disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD) at an adequate dose for an adequate duration:
- Methotrexate: minimum 15 mg/week orally or subcutaneously for at least 3 months (12 weeks)
- Sulfasalazine: minimum 2,000 mg/day for at least 3 months
- Hydroxychloroquine: minimum 400 mg/day for at least 3 months
- Leflunomide: minimum 20 mg/day for at least 3 months
Inadequate response is defined as: persistent moderate-to-severe disease activity (DAS28 >= 3.2 or >= 6 swollen/tender joints) despite adequate DMARD therapy, OR discontinuation due to dose-limiting toxicity or intolerance.
Documentation must include: drug name, dose, duration of therapy, and the specific reason for discontinuation or inadequate response.
Step therapy may be waived if ALL conventional DMARDs are contraindicated with supporting documentation.

---

Criterion 4: Tuberculosis Screening
Latent tuberculosis (TB) screening must have been performed within 12 months prior to initiating adalimumab therapy:
- Negative QuantiFERON-TB Gold (IGRA) test result, OR
- Negative tuberculin skin test (TST/PPD) result, OR
- Documentation of prior treatment for latent TB infection (LTBI)
Members with a positive TB screening must have documentation of appropriate treatment for LTBI prior to initiating adalimumab.
Active tuberculosis is an absolute contraindication to adalimumab therapy.

---

Criterion 5: Absence of Contraindications
The member must NOT have any of the following contraindications to adalimumab:
- Active serious infection, including active tuberculosis, bacterial sepsis, invasive fungal infections, or other opportunistic infections
- New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class III or Class IV congestive heart failure (CHF)
- Demyelinating disease including multiple sclerosis or optic neuritis
- Active hepatitis B infection (HBsAg positive) without concurrent antiviral prophylaxis
- Known hypersensitivity to adalimumab or any component of the formulation
- Active malignancy (other than successfully treated non-melanoma skin cancer)

---

Criterion 6: Combination Therapy Requirement
Adalimumab in combination with methotrexate (10-25 mg/week) is preferred over monotherapy, as evidence demonstrates superior efficacy with combination treatment.
Adalimumab monotherapy is acceptable when methotrexate is contraindicated or not tolerated.
If adalimumab is requested as monotherapy, documentation must include the specific reason methotrexate cannot be co-prescribed (e.g., hepatotoxicity, renal impairment, pregnancy, patient refusal with documented counseling).

---

INITIAL AUTHORIZATION DURATION: 6 months

---

Criterion 7: Continuation — Documented Clinical Response (For Renewals)
For continuation authorization, the member must demonstrate a clinical response to adalimumab therapy:
- At least 20% improvement from baseline in both swollen and tender joint counts (ACR20 response), OR
- Clinically meaningful improvement in DAS28-CRP score (reduction >= 1.2 from baseline), OR
- Physician-documented assessment of meaningful clinical improvement and functional benefit
Failure to achieve clinical response after 6 months of therapy should prompt reassessment of the treatment plan.
Documentation must be submitted with each renewal request.

---

RENEWAL AUTHORIZATION DURATION: 12 months

---

REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION FOR ALL REQUESTS
1. Medical records confirming diagnosis and baseline disease severity (joint counts or DAS28 score)
2. Documentation of prior DMARD therapy: drug name, dose, duration, response, and reason for discontinuation
3. TB screening results within the past 12 months (QuantiFERON or TST result)
4. Prescribing physician specialty and board certification (or rheumatology consultation note)
5. Relevant laboratory results (RF, anti-CCP antibodies, CBC, liver function tests, creatinine)
6. For renewals: most recent clinical assessment documenting response to adalimumab
7. If requesting monotherapy: documentation of methotrexate contraindication or intolerance
